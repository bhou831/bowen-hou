## My Summer Drink Project

This summer, I gave myself a challenge: daily 5k run till September. My route always ends at the Whole Foods near my home, which has turned the chilled drink shelf by the hot bar into the finish line. I arrive sweaty, grab a drink that I have not tried before, finish it while walking home. 

That routine gave me a very specific idea of what makes a good post run drink. I want something cold and crisp. Fizz is optional, but always welcomed. It should replenish some of the liquid I lost without turning into a sugar bomb, and it should not leave that fake sweetener taste (stevia taste) hanging around in my mouth.

![The Whole Foods drink shelf after one of my summer runs](/images/journals/2026-09-02/img.png)

Somehow, the drink shelf these days is packed with probiotic and prebiotic drinks from brands I have never heard of. Most sit in the carefully harmless range of about 20 to 40 calories, low enough that nobody feels guilty, while bacteria inside are allegedly good for the gut. Some even add protein. They are all positioned at eye level, so they are impossible to miss. Meanwhile, they have pushed aside my old favorites, the plain, old fashioned seltzers like La Croix and bubly.

So I decided to try them. Then I kept going. This is my record of the shelf, judged from the perspective of a sweaty runner walking home after a 5K.
